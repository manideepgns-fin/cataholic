Cataholic is a cat that lives on your desktop. This is the first version for Windows: the same cat as the Mac app, drawn in code.

**What she does**
- She falls, lands, and walks about on your windows and on the taskbar.
- Click her to pet her and she purrs. Drag her to carry her somewhere else.
- Eight coats (Ginger, Patches, Tux and more). Pick one from the menu.
- Nine things to do from the menu: say hi, grow big, stretch, yawn, groom, chase her tail, look around, jump, nap. And zoomies.
- "More cats" fills the screen with up to 100 of them.
- "On her own" lets her wander, climb and nap by herself. "Sleep" curls her up and keeps her quiet.
- Sounds, and "At login" to start her with Windows.
- Links from any app or script: `cataholic://sign?count=3&note=Build%20failed` makes her hold up a sign, and `cataholic://zoomies` sends her running.

**Install**
1. Download `Cataholic-win-x64.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Run `Cataholic.exe`. There is no installer, and it needs nothing else installed.
3. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will say it protected your PC the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only do this once.

It runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

**Updates**
She updates herself. About a minute after she starts, and then once a day, she looks here for a newer Windows version. If there is one, she downloads it, checks it, swaps it in and restarts in a couple of seconds. You can also click **Check for updates** in her menu. If she can't write to the folder she lives in (for example Program Files), she leaves herself alone, and you update by downloading the zip again. Keep her in a folder you own, like Documents or your user folder.

**Quit**
Click the paw icon in the tray (bottom right, you may need the little arrow) and choose **Quit**. A right-click on the cat opens the same menu.

**Mac only**
The window grid, where dragging a window shows a grid to snap it into, stays on the Mac. Windows has its own snapping (Snap Layouts, and FancyZones in PowerToys).
