# Cat sounds — all CC0 (public domain): meows from Freesound, hi + purr from BigSoundBank (LaSonotheque)

Replaced the first Wikimedia set on 27 Sep 2026 (owner: "lot of disturbances" — three of those clips were noisy
field recordings). Picked by a measured noise score, then trimmed, faded (8 ms) and loudness-matched; the purr is
an 8 s crossfaded loop. Encoded AAC 160 kbps.

- meow1: "meow-1.wav" by nathan-osman — https://freesound.org/people/nathan-osman/sounds/686775/ — CC0
- meow2: "cat meow" by tuberatanka — https://freesound.org/people/tuberatanka/sounds/110011/ — CC0
- meow3: "Cat meow 1" by fthgurdy — https://freesound.org/people/fthgurdy/sounds/528197/ — CC0
- hi: first mew of "Small mewing of a cat" — https://bigsoundbank.com/small-mewing-of-a-cat-s0098.html — CC0
- purr: 7–15 s of "Cat purring #2" — https://bigsoundbank.com/cat-purring-2-s0981.html — CC0

The three meows were replaced on 13 Oct 2026 (owner: the old ones were "bad quality"), picked by ear from 24 CC0
candidates: trimmed to one meow, high-passed at 70 Hz, 10 ms fade-in / 40 ms fade-out, loudness-matched to the
hi clip (−14.4 LUFS), AAC 160 kbps.
